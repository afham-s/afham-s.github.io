Featured Work

JellyBot 2.0

Soft Robotics & Marine Systems

An ultra-low-cost, biomimetic soft-robotic underwater vehicle designed for non-invasive coral reef surveillance. Engineered with an industrial acrylic pressure hull sealed with marine-grade O-rings and custom 3D-printed flap clamps to drive an 8-tentacle soft pneumatic silicone bell.

Target Cost
~SGD $1,000
Propulsion Speed
3.05 cm/s
Depth Stability
±2 cm
Mission Endurance
3.1 Hours
Key Highlights: Calibrated negative buoyancy for power-free descent to 2–5m; 2-pump pneumatic architecture acting as a mechanical low-pass filter; verified with 2m pool testing and red-dye submersion checks.
